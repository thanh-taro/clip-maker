# Principles

- **PRN-01** — *Private by construction.* Media never leaves the device: no uploads, no analytics,
  no share links. Nothing is kept between sessions. Scratch space used during an export is deleted
  when the export ends. When in doubt, don't send.
- **PRN-02** — *What you see is what you export.* The preview shows exactly what the export will
  contain, frame for frame, apart from resolution.
- **PRN-03** — *Light, not heavy.* Every common edit takes one or two gestures. Good defaults mean
  most clips need no settings. The tool never feels like a full studio.
- **PRN-04** — *Never destructive.* Source files are never changed. Every edit stays editable and
  undoable until the page closes.
- **PRN-05** — *Fits the host.* It looks and behaves like part of the portal when embedded, and
  stays fully usable on its own.
- **PRN-06** — *Works where you are.* The same editing capabilities on desktop and phone, each
  with a layout and input suited to it.
- **PRN-07** — *Honest about limits.* When the device or browser can't do something (a format, a
  size, a speed), the tool says so plainly and offers what it can do. It never fails silently.
