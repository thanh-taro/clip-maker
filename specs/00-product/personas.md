# Personas

### PER-01 — Owner
Taro, a backend developer who built the portal. Records the screen to show a bug or a demo and
needs to cut the dead time, add a label or two and shrink the file before sending it. Works on a
laptop with a keyboard and uses the portal daily. Cares that nothing leaves the machine.

### PER-02 — Short-form creator
Makes vertical clips for social apps from phone footage, often on the phone itself and often in
Vietnamese. Wants a 9:16 frame, trending colour looks, text, music and speed changes. Hates
watermarks and features that suddenly need a subscription.

### PER-03 — Family storyteller
Joins phone videos and photos from a trip or a birthday, adds a title and a song, and shares the
result with family. Not technical. Needs it to be obvious and forgiving: undo everything, never
lose the originals.

### PER-04 — Quick fixer
Has one file and one job: trim it, crop it to a frame, rotate it, mute it, brighten it or make it
small enough to send. Wants it done in under two minutes on whatever device is at hand.
