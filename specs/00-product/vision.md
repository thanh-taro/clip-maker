# Clip Maker — Vision

**Cut, join, caption and score your clips right in the browser. Your video never leaves your
device, with no watermark and no account.**

Clip Maker is a light video editor in the TaroKit portal's Media tools. It covers what most people
do with a mainstream mobile editor (clips on a timeline, text, music, speed, colour and a
short-form frame) without sending one frame anywhere.

## Problem
- Mainstream free editors keep moving basic features, even export, behind subscriptions. Their
  terms leave people unsure who may use what they upload.
- Online editors upload your footage, stamp a watermark on free exports, and cap the resolution
  and length.
- Screen recordings and family footage are often private, and people would rather not upload them.
- A full desktop editor is too much for a two-minute job.

## Who it is for
PER-01 Owner, PER-02 Short-form creator, PER-03 Family storyteller, PER-04 Quick fixer.

## Differentiators
1. **Private by construction.** Everything happens on the device. The tool cannot send data
   anywhere, works offline, and needs no account (PRN-01).
2. **Nothing held back.** No watermark, no paywall, no export cap within the project length limit
   (RULE-03), and export at full quality.
3. **Short-form ready, in two languages.** Frame presets for vertical, square and wide video,
   trending colour looks, and an English/Vietnamese UI on desktop and phone (PRN-06).

## Non-goals
- Accounts, sync, sharing links, collaboration, cloud storage.
- Analytics, ads, monetization.
- Automatic captions and other AI features.
- Templates, sticker or effect marketplaces, stock media libraries.
- Keyframe animation in M-1 and M-2 (an M-3 candidate).
- Fetching anything from the network while working, including music.
- Keeping projects between sessions in M-1.

## Success
M-1 succeeds when the Owner uses Clip Maker instead of a mainstream editor for their own screen
recordings and short clips. The measurable bar is in `60-delivery/roadmap.md`.
