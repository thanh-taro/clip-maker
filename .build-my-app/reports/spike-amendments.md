# ADR amendments queued from spikes (apply at G3)
- **MOD-Engine:** a frame lasts until the next frame's start. Never use rounded durations. (SP-compositor)
- **ADR-0010:** remove the encoder's priming delay through an MP4 edit list (AAC, 2112 samples measured). (SP-compositor)
- **ADR-0004:** the trial encode measures encoder delay rather than assuming it. (SP-compositor)
- **ADR-0004:** probe only a minimal set at start-up (≤ 300 ms); probe the full size × rate grid when the export panel opens. Cap concurrency at 4. Normalise the AAC description to its 2-byte AudioSpecificConfig. WebKit's FLAC decode is a false positive. (SP-codec)
- **ADR-0009:** add a cache of intermediate decoded frames for long-GOP backward scrubbing, or document the worst case. WebKit's decoder needs flushes. (SP-compositor)
- **ADR-0010 and SP-storage:** WebKit gives the embedded frame 1/10 of standalone quota, so no_space is a real path. Scratch names are unique per session. The start-up sweep skips files still in use, because Chrome shares storage between same-site embedded and standalone. (SP-frame)
- **Audio:** bound the wait on WebKit's AudioContext.resume(). (SP-frame)
- **ADR-0005:** mediabunny does not throw on a missing moov, so MOD-Container maps "no tracks / 0 packets" to a typed error. (SP-license)
- **Audio ADR and ADR-0010 (from SP-audio):**
  - The limiter ceiling is −1.5 dBTP internally (BS.1770 meter, 4× side-chain).
  - AAC and Opus exports need a verify-and-trim pass: decode, check the true peak, and re-encode with a trim if it is above −1.3 dBTP (at most 3 passes).
  - Opus priming is 312 samples, removed via the edit list.
  - The worklet ring buffer is 400 ms.
  - The Web Audio graph variant is rejected.
  - WebKit's Opus encode overshoots by about 4 dB and its AAC encoder ignores `bitrate`: record both in the capability probe.
- **RULE-08 / F-007.AC2 (spec change, owner):** check the ramp bars on the high-precision render (CPU reference + half-float layer), at Grain 0, with Vignette checked per position. On 8-bit outputs, allow no reversal above ΔL* 0.5 and require ΔE00 ≤ 1 against the reference. 8-bit and limited-range outputs can't keep 256 distinct steps. (SP-gpu-color)
- **ADR-0006 §3.2:** use a half-float intermediate (renderable in all 3 engines), with an 8-bit fallback. Drop recompute-taps. (SP-gpu-color)
- **ADR-0007 / ADR-0010:** encoders differ: Chrome writes limited range, WebKit and openh264 write full range, and all tag transfer as sRGB (13). MOD-Export writes the `colr` tags explicitly. The owner's player check decides the transfer tag and whether we convert to limited range ourselves. (SP-gpu-color)
- **ADR-0007 §3 / ADR-0013:** a look is a 33³ 8-bit PNG of 4–15 KB measured, against a budget of 30 KB per look. The build rejects a look over 108 KB or with storage error above ΔE00 1. (SP-gpu-color)
- **platform-mapping.md (RULE-22):** slow device = the start-up effects check (full chain + look + Blur, 1080p, 30 frames) renders below 30 fps. (SP-gpu-color)
- **ADR-0016:** grade fidelity fixtures are RGB stills; the oracle is the CPU reference in float. (SP-gpu-color)
- **ADR-0008 (from SP-timestretch):** adopt our own WSOLA and reject the phase vocoder.
  - §4: a pinned frame every 1 s replaces the cross-fade.
  - §6: after a seek, decode from the previous sync point (up to 4 s of source at 4×).
  - Known limits: doubled attacks at 0.25×; some very short clicks lost at 2–4×; 3–7 ms timing jitter.
- **ADR-0016:** WSOLA golden hashes hold across engines, so use them as test oracles.
- **ADR-0010 / MOD-Export / MOD-Audio:** decode the clips' sound before the video loop starts, then run mix + limiter + AAC + verify in the audio worker alongside the video. Never run the audio pass serially before the video. The streaming writer must accept audio that arrives after the video. (SP-speed)
- **ADR-0008 (verify-and-trim):** aim the trim at −1.5 dBTP decoded using the measured gain→peak slope (secant step), and stop at ≤ −1.0 dBTP (NFR). The fixed step needed 3 passes in both engines and ended at −2.58 (Chrome) and −1.18 (WebKit). (SP-speed)
- **ADR-0004 §5:** keep `no-preference` and `latencyMode: 'quality'`, with an encoder queue cap of 4–8. Software H.264 is ≈ 0.8× at 1080p on the laptop, so the probe records hardware vs software and the export estimate uses it. Consider 720p as the default on slow devices. (SP-speed)
- **ADR-0006 §7:** `VideoFrame(canvas)` is the export path. `readPixels` read-back doubles export time, and `transferToImageBitmap` → `VideoFrame` fails in Chrome. WebKit's `VideoFrame(canvas)` costs ≈ 8 ms per frame. (SP-speed)
- **ADR-0016:** take NFR-04 timings on a quiet host and record the load average. (SP-speed)
- **ADR-0010 / MOD-Export (from SP-storage):**
  - **Deletion rule:** after a successful save, delete the scratch file 10 s after the click. On fail or cancel, close the handle and delete at once. At the next export or New project, delete only files whose 10 s are up. Deleting on `pagehide` is best effort only.
  - **Sweep:** each session holds the Web Lock `clipmaker-scratch:<session>`. A file `export-<sid>-*.part` from another session is deleted only if `locks.request(ifAvailable)` grants that session's lock.
  - **Heartbeats:** rejected.
  - **Writes:** use 1 MB chunks.
- **Spec change (owner approval at G3):**
  - F-015.R7 and INV-01: replace "at the latest when the page closes" with "or, if the page closes first, the next time the tool opens".
  - F-015.AC7: inspect storage at least 10 s after the save.
- **ADR-0010, rate control (from SP-size-target):**
  - **Estimate:** use a calibration of 3 × 2 s, capped at the nominal bitrate. It measured within 6 % in Chrome and 14 % in WebKit, inside the ±25 % target. 3 × 1 s fails.
  - **Feasibility pre-check:** calibrate at the target bitrate before exporting. If the encoder can't reach that rate (encoders have content-dependent minimums of about 0.6/1.4/2.6 Mb/s at 480/720/1080 for high motion), treat the target as unmet at that resolution and follow RULE-15's offer path. When the rate is reachable, overshoot is at most 0.8 %, and one corrective pass fixes it.
  - **Floors:** 300/600/1000 kbps, pending the owner's visual check.
  - **480p width:** 848, not 854, because Chrome resamples 854.
  - No spec change needed: RULE-15's "met only if the bitrate ≥ floor" already covers this; the floor becomes max(table floor, measured encoder minimum).
- **ADR-0004 (owner run of SP-codec):**
  - Desktop Safari 27's H.264 encoder produces nothing in `latencyMode:'quality'`, but works in `'realtime'` (3.9× real time at 1080p30).
  - The trial encode, and every export, falls back from quality to realtime when no output arrives within 2 s. The probe records the mode per engine.
  - SP-size-target accuracy must be re-checked in realtime mode on Safari (QA).
- **ADR-0011 / MOD-Audio (from SP-frame on iPhone):** serve AudioWorklet modules with `Access-Control-Allow-Origin` for the tool's own origin. iOS standalone refused the same-origin worklet over CORS. Check again in QA.
- **ADR-0010 §8 (from SP-ios):** iOS suspends hidden pages, so the "hidden + 5 s" rule can't fire. Add a rule: no new export progress within 3 s of the page becoming visible again means interrupted. Show COPY-error.export_interrupted at once and clean up behind it (measured 3.1 s to the message, 5 s to clean-up). An export that is still alive on return resumes.
- **Portal change request (from SP-ios):** add `web-share` to the embed frame's `allow`, so the tool can offer Share and Save to Photos when embedded. RULE-17 gains web-share as an optional grant.
- **MOD-Engine / ADR-0009 (from SP-hdr and SP-mem-phone, real iPhone HEVC):**
  - Take the wanted frame's presentation time from the container index (`getPacket(t + 2 µs)`), never from packet durations. HEVC B-frame reordering otherwise picks frames 1–3 early (NFR-08).
  - After a random-access restart, drop open-GOP leading pictures (pts < keyframe pts). WebKit errors and closes the decoder otherwise.
  - The decode look-ahead is 12 frames.
- **ADR-0007 §4 (SP-hdr):** HDR frames are opaque (`format: null`) in Chrome and WebKit, so the own 10-bit shader cannot be fed.
  - Use the engine's conversion (`texImage2D(VideoFrame)`) in both preview and export. Preview vs export measured ΔE00 ≤ 0.43 mean and ≤ 1.16 max; Chrome vs WebKit 0.13 mean.
  - The probe records opaque HDR frames.
  - WebKit's HDR upload costs about 25 ms per 4K frame. iPhone fps is pending.
- **ADR-0006 / ADR-0009 (SP-mem-phone):** size grade intermediates to the placed output size, not the source size. Allow the 8-bit intermediate on phones at 4K. The 4K project otherwise holds 318 MB of textures.
- **RULE-02 / D-012 (SP-mem-phone):** 5120 × 2880 sources preview below 24 fps on the M3 (14 fps in Chrome). Record this as a known limit; export stays correct.
- **ADR-0010 (SP-mem-phone):** Playwright WebKit cannot open an OPFS sync access handle, so automated WebKit tests need a null sink. Confirm OPFS on iOS (owner).

- **ADR-0007 §4 (SP-hdr, iPhone):**
  - iOS decodes HLG HEVC to 8-bit NV12 tagged sRGB full range, so the decoder tone-maps.
  - Use the engine path everywhere: iPhone preview vs export ΔE00 mean 0.42, max 1.40.
  - Trust the frame's colour tag over the track's.
- **ADR-0009 decoder budget (SP-mem-phone, iPhone):**
  - On iOS the 2nd concurrent 4K HEVC decoder failed (`Decoder failure`). Until `dec.html` says otherwise, the budget is 1 concurrent 4K decoder on phones.
  - Cross-fades between 4K clips decode the outgoing overlap first into an output-size frame cache.
  - Catch `Decoder failure` on a 2nd session and fall back to sequential decoding.
- **MOD-Engine playback (SP-mem-phone, iPhone):** when decode is slower than real time, never restart from a keyframe while the target is ahead of the decoder. Decode forward and show the newest frame. Sources that decode below real time are "slow" (RULE-22 notice).
- **MOD-Export (SP-mem-phone, iPhone):**
  - The iOS H.264 encoder can stop draining for more than 4 s mid-export. Use a long stall watchdog (≥ 20 s), measure output gaps, and on a real stall re-create the encoder at the next keyframe.
  - `VideoFrame(canvas)` costs 51 ms per frame at 3322 × 2160 on the iPhone, so default exports of above-1080p sources to ≤ 1080p to meet NFR-04.
  - Errors are reported with `name: message`, because WebKit's `stack` omits the message.
- **ADR-0006 / MOD-Render (from SP-ios on iPhone):** after a long lock (60 s), iOS restarted the GPU process, and the next export's new WebGL2 context was lost at frame 1. The engine must handle `webglcontextlost` at every stage: recreate the context and re-upload resources (shaders, LUTs, text textures), retrying up to 5 times 400 ms apart before failing. Only a loss mid-export fails that export (COPY-error.export_failed), and preview always recovers.
