## Bundled music (QA batch 2, 2026-11-20)
- The music pack is nine CC0 1.0 tracks from Free Music Archive (Loyalty Freak Music, HoliznaCC0, Komiku),
  each cut to its first 60 s (one to 75 s) with a fade-out. CC0 means no attribution is required, but the
  tool credits every track anyway (title, author, source page and licence in the Music picker and in the
  licence notices).
- CC0 is the uploader's own dedication. It cannot stop a third party's automated content-ID system (for
  example on video platforms) from flagging an export that uses a bundled track; such a claim can usually
  be disputed by pointing to the track's page. The saved track pages and download record are kept in
  `licences/music/`.
