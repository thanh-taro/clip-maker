## Gaps and risks

1. Piano: no FMA track with a CC0 licence and a confirmed piano sound was found (the piano tracks that surfaced were CC BY-NC / NC-ND). Options to review, none verified by me: the Internet Archive "Musopen - The Complete Chopin Collection" (listed CC0 1.0 per search snippet; page not fetched); Wikimedia Commons Musopen files show "Public Domain Mark", not CC0, which the policy may not accept; OpenGameArt Yoiyami ambient piano (CC0 per search snippet; OpenGameArt returned HTTP 403 so I could not verify). Public-domain composition does not make a recording public domain, so only the recording's own dedication counts.
2. FMA boilerplate on every album says "Please check individual tracks for their respective licensing info"; so the licence column comes from each track's own page, and all 12 listed tracks were fetched individually and show CC0.
3. "Instrumental: No" appears on many Loyalty Freak/Komiku/Monplaisir pages even where album tags say Instrumental; probably an unset flag but listen for vocals.
4. Content ID: an FMA comment on Komiku's "Mall" reports a YouTube copyright claim. CC0 does not prevent third-party automated claims; users posting exports could be hit. Not a licence defect, but note it in user-facing docs. Do not ship "Mall".
5. Avoid: HoliznaCC0 items under the separate "holiznapatreon" account (e.g. Orphaned Media, Summer Series), remixes/covers (e.g. "Phonk Remix", "House Of The Rising Sun"), and anything where the track page licence is missing.
6. CC0 is the uploader's declaration; FMA does not verify sample provenance. Keep the page URL, retrieval date and a saved copy of the licence text in the asset register for each track. Not legal advice; legal review recommended for the Monplaisir track and for the artist-declaration approach in general.
7. Size: 8 tracks x ~60 s at AAC 128 kbps is about 8 MB, within the 10 MB budget; 12 tracks would be about 12 MB, so trim alternates to ~45 s if used.
