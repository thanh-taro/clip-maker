# Spec questions from the build, for the owner at the wave review
From E1 (MOD-Core/Project/Plan). The behaviour currently built is in [brackets].
1. **F-003.AC8:** 9 min 59 s plus 1 s is exactly 600 s, which RULE-03 allows. Proposal: change the AC to 9 min 59.5 s, as F-009.AC6 already uses. [The AC is not cited by a test; the 59.5 s case is tested under RULE-03.]
2. **RULE-03 vs RULE-05:** RULE-03 accepts an add or duplicate when one frame fits, but RULE-05 sets a 0.1 s minimum length. [Refused when less than the minimum fits.] Proposal: make RULE-03 say "when less than the minimum length fits".
3. **Frame-rate change with adjacent audio clips:** after rounding to the new frame grid they can overlap. [The later clip moves to the next free frame start.] Proposal: add this to RULE-04.
4. **Text fade default** is not stated. [Off.] Proposal: state it in RULE-11.
5. **Cross-fade between clips too short to hold 0.1 s:** no behaviour is specified. [Nothing happens.] Proposal: the cross-fade control is unavailable, with a reason.
6. **Speed change stopped by the minimum length:** no notice is specified. [None shown.] Proposal: show COPY-notice.length_limited, or a new "too short for that speed" notice.
7. **RULE-02 "too short":** it refuses sources shorter than one frame, but sources between 1 frame and 0.1 s become 2-frame clips, which breaks RULE-05's 0.1 s minimum. (From the E1 review.) Proposal: refuse sources shorter than the 0.1 s minimum, rounded up to whole frames, with COPY-reason.too_short. [Built: sources under 0.125 s are refused (0.1 s rounded up at 24 fps, so a later frame-rate change cannot break the minimum). Pending your OK.]

## Technical items from E4 (MOD-Audio). Claude decides these; they are listed for transparency and accepted as built.
8. **MOD-Audio surface `render(audioPlan, fromSample, count)`:** a block plan holds only the voices sounding in that block, but the limiter's 1 s pre-roll needs the voices before it. [Built as `render(voices, sources, endSample, from, count)` plus a streaming `Mixer` and `renderOffline`.] Proposal: update MOD-Audio's public surface.
9. **Verify-and-trim (ADR-0008 outcome rule 2):** "first step = the excess + 0.2 dB" does not say excess over what; "at most 3 passes" does not say if the first encode counts; "a fixed −3 dB trim" does not say relative to what. [Excess over the −1.5 dBTP aim; the first encode counts, so at most 2 trims; the fallback is the last gain − 3 dB, encoded once more and not measured.] Proposal: write these into the ADR outcome or MOD-Audio.
10. **Clock:** ADR-0008 §5 says "`getOutputTimestamp()` minus `outputLatency`", but the output timestamp already describes what reaches the speakers. [The output timestamp is used, carried forward to now; `currentTime − outputLatency − baseLatency` only when there is no timestamp.] Proposal: write it this way in platform-mapping.
11. **Limiter margin:** the −1.5 dBTP ceiling holds on the limiter's own side-chain. Independent meters read up to −1.46 dBTP (BS.1770-4) and −1.39 dBTP (a 64-tap meter) on synthetic +6 dBTP overs. This matches the ADR's "PCM ≤ −1.35 dBTP" and stays inside RULE-12's −1 dBTP. [Tests check ≤ −1.35 on the meters.] No change proposed.
12. **"Underruns counted in debug builds":** there is no debug build flag. [Always counted; it is cheap. The stats come back only on request.] Proposal: drop "in debug builds".

*Items 8–12 applied 2026-11-05 to MOD-Audio, ADR-0008 and platform-mapping (11 needed no change).*

## Items from E5 (MOD-Export/MOD-Scratch). Technical ones are decided by Claude and listed for transparency; behaviour ones need your OK.
32. **Late sound in the muxer (ADR-0010 outcome item 1, risk 5) — mediabunny alone does not allow it.** Its writer interleaves tracks by time and queues every sample in memory until each track has one (`interleaveSamples`, in every `fastStart` mode, fragmented too), so writing all video before the sound would hold the encoded video in memory. [Built: MOD-Export's mux schedule writes video packets that come before the verified sound to a **spill file** (a second scratch file, same `export-<session>-<random>.part` naming, so the discard and sweep rules cover it); when the sound arrives it replays them and feeds both tracks in time order, so the writer queues at most one sound packet's worth of video. MP4 is written with `fastStart: false` (index at the end). Proven in `tests/e2e/export.spec.ts` "ADR-0010 outcome item 1 …": all video spilled, one packet held at a time, the sink receives the media before the index.] The room check stays `estimate × 1.3`; in the worst case (sound later than all video) the spill adds the video size again, briefly. Proposal: name the spill file in ADR-0010 outcome item 1 / MOD-Scratch, and decide whether the room check should be `estimate × 1.3 + video estimate`.
33. **Clip sound for exports is not decoded by the export-audio worker yet.** Windowed decoding and 48 kHz resampling (MOD-Audio, ADR-0008 §2/§6) are not built, and the export-audio worker's 10 KB budget cannot hold the container reader (≈ 73 KB). [Built: the export-audio worker takes decoded 48 kHz stereo PCM per source (`PcmSource`, the same shape as the audio worker's `audio:pcm`); whoever decodes for the preview feeds both. Measured bundle 7.7 KB.] Proposal: when windowed decoding is built, the export worker (which has the reader) sends the clips' encoded sound packets to the export-audio worker over the port, which decodes them with `AudioDecoder` before the video loop starts (ADR-0010 outcome item 1 order kept).
34. **Square frames vs "long side a multiple of 16" (ADR-0010 outcome item 9).** A 1:1 frame at 1080 cannot be square with a long side that is a multiple of 16. [Built: square frames stay square at the resolution (1080 × 1080); every other aspect gets an even short side = the resolution and a long side rounded to a multiple of 16, e.g. 4:5 at 1080 = 1080 × 1344, 9:16 = 1080 × 1920.] Proposal: say so in architecture.md "Export tables".
35. **Stall watchdog action (ADR-0010 outcome item 2).** "Re-create the encoder and restart at the next output keyframe" needs the frames that were sent but not yet output, which would mean holding up to the encoder's look-ahead of canvas-backed frames (GPU memory on phones). [Built: the watchdog records the longest output gap (reported in `ExportStats`) and, after 20 s without output while frames wait, fails the export with COPY-error.export_failed (`EncoderStall: …`). Risk 3 is retired (longest gap 5.8 s in the 10-min iPhone run).] Proposal: accept fail-on-stall for M-1, or keep the restart rule and budget the held frames.
36. **The shared final gain (ADR-0008 outcome item 2) applies to lossy exports only.** [The first lossy export of a mix runs verify-and-trim; a later lossy export of the same mix (video or audio-only) encodes once at that gain. WAV is the limiter's output unscaled (it is lossless and already ≤ −1.5 dBTP).] Please confirm WAV should not take the gain.
37. **Size-target corrective pass.** [It rewrites the same scratch file from byte 0 (sync handle `truncate`) and re-decodes from the start; the progress total doubles, so the bar keeps rising (monotone).] Decided.
38. **The export-audio worker moved from `src/audio` to `src/export`.** MOD-Audio is "not responsible for encoding or writing files"; the worker encodes, decodes and measures, so it belongs to MOD-Export and uses MOD-Audio's mixer, limiter, meter and `verifyAndTrim`. MOD-Audio gained `TruePeakMeter` (block-wise BS.1770 meter, equal to `truePeak` over the whole signal; needed so a 10-minute decode is never held). Decided.
39. **Colour range tag "as produced" (ADR-0007 outcome item 2).** [Primaries, matrix and transfer are tagged bt709; `fullRange` is taken from the encoder's own decoder-config metadata, and is `false` (limited) when the encoder gives none.] If WebKit's encoder writes full range but reports nothing, the tag would say limited. Open: the owner's player check (ADR-0007 outcome) should include a WebKit export.
40. **Library names in mandatory fields (RULE-15 Contents).** mediabunny fills the MP4 `hdlr` name field ("MediabunnyVideoHandler"/"…SoundHandler") and WebM's mandatory MuxingApp/WritingApp with its name. Creation times are zeroed. [Kept: the fields are mandatory and the library is unmodified (D-042).] Please confirm, or we write empty names (needs a library option or a post-write patch).
41. **Abort answer before terminate.** Terminating the export worker does not release its sync access handle at once, so an immediate delete failed in Chromium. [Built per ADR-0010 outcome item 10: the result is reported at once (cancel ≤ 1 s); behind it the worker is asked to close and delete its files (`export:abort` → `export:aborted`), is terminated after the answer or 1.5 s, and the main thread deletes again.] Decided.
