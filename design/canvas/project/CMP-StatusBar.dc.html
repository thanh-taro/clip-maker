Your media never leaves this device

Clip Maker edits and exports entirely in your browser. Nothing is uploaded and your project isn't kept after you close the page. The first visit downloads the tool's looks, fonts and music (about 12 MB) so it works offline. During an export it may use temporary space on your device. That space is deleted when the export ends, or the next time you open Clip Maker if the page closed during an export.
