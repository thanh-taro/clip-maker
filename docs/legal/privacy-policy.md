# Privacy Policy — Clip Maker

**Effective date:** [date of publication]
**Operator:** TaroKit ("we", "us")
**Contact:** CONTACT_EMAIL

Clip Maker is a video editor that runs in your web browser at `https://clip-maker.tarokit.app`, on its own or inside the TaroKit portal (`https://tarokit.app`). This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it.

## 1. The short version

Clip Maker works entirely on your device. Your videos, photos, music, text and projects are never uploaded to us or to anyone else. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no cookies and no trackers. We do not collect personal data.

## 2. Information we collect

We do not collect, receive or store any personal data through Clip Maker. In particular:

- the media you open, the projects you make and the files you export stay on your device;
- there is no sign-in, so we hold no name, email address or password;
- there are no analytics, crash reports, error reports, cookies or device fingerprinting.

## 3. What is kept on your device

To work, the tool uses your browser's own storage. None of it is sent to us.

| Where | What | How long |
|---|---|---|
| Page memory | Your current project, opened media, preview, undo history, theme and language | Until you close the page |
| Browser private file storage (`clip-maker-scratch/`) | Temporary parts of a video while it is being exported | Deleted when the export finishes. If the page closed during an export, deleted the next time you open Clip Maker in the same browser |
| Offline copy (`clip-maker-offline-<version>`) | The tool's own program files (about 12 MB), so it can open without a connection. No personal data | Until a new version replaces it, the browser removes it, or you clear the site's data |
| Tab lock | A marker that a Clip Maker tab is open | Until the tab closes |

Clip Maker does not use cookies, local storage, session storage or IndexedDB. Your project is not saved between visits: export your video before closing the page.

## 4. Network connections

Clip Maker downloads only its own files from its own address, over HTTPS. None of these requests carry your media, your project or any other personal information, and the tool's security settings block connections to any other server.

**Hosting.** The tool is served by Cloudflare, Inc., our hosting provider. Like every website host, Cloudflare automatically processes technical request data (such as your IP address, browser type, the time and the file requested) to deliver the files and protect the service against abuse. We have not turned on any analytics or log-collection features, and we do not receive or use this data. Cloudflare handles it as described in its own privacy policy (`https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/`).

**Inside the TaroKit portal.** When Clip Maker is embedded in the portal, it receives only the portal's theme and language, and sends back one message saying it is ready. That message contains no content about you or your work.

**Things you choose to do.**
- *Download* saves the exported file to your device.
- *Share* (where your device supports it) hands the file to your device's share menu; where it goes from there is your choice and is governed by the app you pick.
- Music source links and the source-code link open other websites in a new tab without telling them where you came from. Those sites' own privacy policies apply.

## 5. Legal basis and your rights

Because we do not collect or process personal data about you, there is no personal data for us to access, correct, delete, restrict or transfer, and no consent is requested.

- **Vietnam.** We do not process personal data within the meaning of Decree No. 13/2023/ND-CP on Personal Data Protection. If that changes, we will update this policy before any processing starts and ask for consent where the law requires it.
- **European Economic Area and United Kingdom.** If the GDPR or UK GDPR applies to you, the same is true: we do not process your personal data. Technical request data processed by our hosting provider is handled under that provider's terms for security and delivery of the service.

You can remove everything Clip Maker has stored in your browser at any time by clearing the site's data in your browser settings.

## 6. Children

Clip Maker can be used by anyone and does not collect personal data from anyone, including children. Children under 16 should use the tool with permission from a parent or guardian, in line with the TaroKit portal's own rules.

## 7. Security

Your files never leave your device, so they are protected by your device and browser. The tool is served only over HTTPS, with strict security headers that stop it from contacting other servers or being embedded by sites other than the TaroKit portal.

## 8. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will publish the new version at the same place with a new effective date. If a change ever means that we would start collecting personal data, we will say so clearly in the tool before it takes effect.
