# Clip Maker 1.2.0 — 2026-11-25

## New: colour tools
Four new groups in Adjust, after Effects, for video and photo clips:
- **Colour mixer** — shift the hue, saturation and lightness of one of eight colour ranges (red, orange, yellow, green, aqua, blue, purple, magenta): a deeper blue sky, autumn leaves, calmer skin tones.
- **Colour pop** — keep one colour range and turn everything else black and white.
- **Split toning** — tint the shadows and the highlights with two colours, with a balance between them.
- **Duotone** — recolour the picture in two colours, with six built-in pairs or your own.

Each tool can be reset on its own; Reset all, Copy, Paste, Apply to all and Hold to compare include them. The export matches the preview. Works with a keyboard and screen readers, and on phones.

Verified by automated tests in two desktop browser engines (Chromium and WebKit) only. Device checks (iPhone, Android, Firefox, screen reader, timings on reference devices) were not run; the phone speed of the colour tools is an estimate from desktop measurements.
